### The three source collections

BreastDCEDL is itself an aggregation. It pools three public collections held by
[The Cancer Imaging Archive](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/), and credit is owed
to each of them independently of the aggregation:

| Collection | What it is | Patients here |
|---|---|---:|
| **I-SPY1** (ACRIN 6657) | multi-centre neoadjuvant trial, enrolled 2002 to 2006 | 167 |
| **I-SPY2** | multi-centre neoadjuvant trial, enrolled 2010 to 2016 | 982 |
| **Duke-Breast-Cancer-MRI** | single-institution retrospective series, Duke Hospital, 2000 to 2014 | 914 |

The Duke collection is also described in:

> Saha, A., Harowicz, M.R., Grimm, L.J., et al. *A machine learning approach to
> radiogenomics of breast cancer: a study of 922 subjects and 529 DCE-MRI features.*
> British Journal of Cancer **119**(4), 508–516 (2018).

Each collection has its own TCIA landing page carrying its citation and its licence.
Cite all three alongside BreastDCEDL, not BreastDCEDL alone.

### Changes were made

This is the part of the CC BY condition that is easiest to overlook, so it is stated
explicitly. **The images in this repository are not the released volumes.** Each one has
been transformed as follows, and the complete procedure is in
[`notebooks/02_build_dataset.ipynb`](notebooks/02_build_dataset.ipynb):

- an 80 mm **physical** window is cropped around the tumour, so that tumour size is
  preserved rather than normalised away
- intensities are min-max normalised over the **whole volume**, all phases and all
  slices together, rather than per slice as the original authors do
- the result is resized to 224 x 224 pixels at a constant 0.357 mm per pixel
- eight slices are retained per patient, evenly spaced after trimming 15% from each end
  of the lesion, rather than every tumour-bearing slice
- the pre-contrast, early post-contrast and late post-contrast acquisitions are fused
  into the red, green and blue channels of a single 8-bit PNG

Two patients of the release were dropped because the metadata bounding box does not map
onto the MinCrop volume, and further patients were dropped for lacking a subtype label
or imaging, leaving 2,063 of the original 2,072.
